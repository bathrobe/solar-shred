# Project Scope

## Project Name Candidates

- Solar Shred
- Sunward Skate
- Orbit Grind

## One-Line Summary

A compact tactical RPG prototype where a player rides a skateboard-space-shuttle through a solar route, wins trick battles, and upgrades their craft to reach a treasure in the Sun.

## Target User

Players who enjoy tactical RPG choices, skateboarding style, and a short, self-contained game loop.

## Problem

Most short web games ask players either to make tactical choices or to chase a style fantasy. This prototype combines both: trick selection drives a readable battle system, while upgrades make the route toward the Sun feel earned.

## Core Workflow

1. Start at the outer edge of the solar route in a skateboard-space-shuttle.
2. Enter a tactical skate battle against a rival skater or environmental obstacle.
3. Choose a trick each turn; compare its score and effect against the opponent's move.
4. Win the battle to earn Stardust.
5. Spend Stardust on a heat-shield upgrade.
6. Advance toward the Sun and complete a final treasure encounter.

## What We Are Building

- A single-page JavaScript game prototype.
- A compact route with three readable stages: outer system, inner system, and the Sun.
- One tactical battle type, presented as a game of SKATE or an obstacle duel.
- Three player tricks with distinct scores or effects.
- One rival/obstacle opponent with simple deterministic or lightly randomized moves.
- Stardust rewards and one upgrade that visibly unlocks the next stage.
- A short ending state that reveals the Sun-core treasure.

## What We Are Not Building

- A free-roaming 3D solar system.
- Separate explorable levels for every planet.
- Real-time skateboarding physics or animation.
- Multiple playable characters, classes, inventory systems, or a quest log.
- Online multiplayer or a complete Final Fantasy-scale combat system.
- Procedural content or a large narrative campaign.

## Inspiration And References

- Final Fantasy-style command selection and readable turn outcomes.
- Jet Set Radio-style skate identity and expressive trick names.
- Solar Ash-style cosmic traversal energy.

## Demo Path

1. Open the game and see the Sunward route and the skateboard-shuttle.
2. Start a skate battle, choose tricks, and defeat a rival or obstacle.
3. Collect Stardust and buy the heat-shield upgrade.
4. Advance through the route to the Sun.
5. Reveal the treasure ending.

## Submission Story

Solar Shred is a tiny, focused playable loop: choose tricks tactically, win a skate battle, upgrade a skateboard-space-shuttle, and ride toward a solar treasure. The prototype favors clarity and completion over a sprawling space RPG.
